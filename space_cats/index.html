Space Cats

A pixel-art arcade fighting game for Android, by Gausaa Games

Eight street-fighting cats battle across six arenas in a neon galaxy, with combos, supers and simple touch controls. Play Arcade, Survival, the Daily Challenge, Versus and Wi-Fi battles between two phones. No ads, no in-app purchases and no account needed; achievements and leaderboards with Google Play Games, if you like.
