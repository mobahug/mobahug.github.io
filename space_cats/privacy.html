Space Cats Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Space Cats is a game by Gausaa Games ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information about you.

No personal data comes to us

We receive no personal data from Space Cats. The game has:

  • no ads and no advertising ID,
  • no analytics or tracking of its own,
  • no accounts of its own,
  • no in-app purchases,
  • no server of ours: the game never sends anything to us.

What can leave your device is described below: your achievements and scores, only if you use Google Play Games, and what a Wi-Fi battle sends to the other player's device.

Google Play Games (optional)

On Android, Space Cats uses Google Play Games Services for achievements and leaderboards. You can play the whole game without it.

  • Signing in. If you have a Google Play Games profile, Play Games may sign you in when the game starts. If you are not signed in, the game works just the same, and you can sign in later from the shop's MISSIONS or RECORDS tab.
  • What is sent. While you are signed in, the game sends Google your achievements (the missions you finish and your progress towards them) and your scores on three leaderboards (arcade high score, best survival run and best combo), together with your Play Games player ID. The Play Games software in the game also sends Google technical information (diagnostics) to keep the service working.
  • Who receives it. This information goes to Google, which runs Google Play Games under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive it: we only see the leaderboards, like any player, and totals such as how many players earned an achievement.
  • Who sees it. Your Play Games name, avatar, achievements and scores can be seen by other players as your Play Games profile settings allow (everyone, friends only, or only you).
  • Your choices. You can play without signing in. You can delete your Play Games data for Space Cats, or your whole Play Games profile, in your Google Play Games profile settings or your Google Account.

Wi-Fi battles

WI-FI BATTLE lets two players fight each other on two devices on the same local network (the same Wi-Fi, or one device sharing its hotspot). It only connects when you choose HOST or JOIN, and only directly to the other player's device at the address you enter. The two devices exchange only what the fight needs: button presses, fighter picks, the match settings, the game version and the look each player chose (title, avatar frame, aura colours, skin). None of it is stored by the game or sent anywhere else.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings and progress (coins, unlocked items, records and missions) in files on your device. These files never leave the device through the game; only the achievements and scores described under Google Play Games are sent to Google, while you are signed in. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include them in your own Google backup so your progress can come back on a new phone; that backup belongs to your Google account and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game deletes these files.

Permissions

The game uses three Android permissions:

  • vibration, for short feedback when you press a touch button. You can turn it off in the game: Settings > Controls > Vibration;
  • network access (Android's "internet" permission), for WI-FI BATTLE, to connect to the other player's device on your local network, and for Google Play Games while you are signed in;
  • keeping the Wi-Fi awake (Android's "wake lock" permission), only while a WI-FI BATTLE is connected, so the Wi-Fi answers quickly instead of saving power between button presses.

Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Google Play Games, if used, is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date and in the game (Settings > Game > Privacy Policy).
