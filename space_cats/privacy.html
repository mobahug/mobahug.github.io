Space Cats Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Space Cats is a game by Gausaa Games ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information about you. To delete your data, see Deleting your data.

What comes to us

Space Cats has:

  • no ads except rewarded videos you choose to watch (see Rewarded videos): Google, which shows them, uses your device's advertising ID,
  • no analytics or tracking of our own,
  • no in-app purchases,
  • no e-mail addresses, phone numbers, contacts, location or device identifiers collected by us, and a name only if you use Google Play Games and ONLINE (the name on your Play Games profile: see Your Gausaa Games account).

The only thing that comes to us is what ONLINE play needs, and only if you open ONLINE: an online account with no sign-in, its ranked fights and your best scores (see Online play), and, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, the link between your Play Games account and your one Gausaa Games account (see Your Gausaa Games account); and a copy of your progress, only if you turn the cloud save on (see Cloud save). The rest of what can leave your device is described below: your achievements and scores, only if you use Google Play Games, what Google's ad software collects for the rewarded videos, and what a Wi-Fi battle sends to the other player's device.

Google Play Games (optional)

On Android, Space Cats uses Google Play Games Services for achievements and leaderboards. You can play the whole game without it.

  • Signing in. If you have a Google Play Games profile, Play Games may sign you in when the game starts. If you are not signed in, the game works just the same, and you can sign in later from PROGRESS (the trophy in the main menu), on its MISSIONS or RECORDS tab.
  • What is sent. While you are signed in, the game sends Google your achievements (the missions you finish and your progress towards them) and your scores on three leaderboards (arcade high score, best survival run and best combo), together with your Play Games player ID. The Play Games software in the game also sends Google technical information (diagnostics) to keep the service working.
  • Who receives it. This information goes to Google, which runs Google Play Games under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive it: we only see the leaderboards, like any player, and totals such as how many players earned an achievement. If you open ONLINE while signed in, Google tells our Gausaa ID service two ids of your Play Games account and its Play Games name, nothing else (see Your Gausaa Games account).
  • Who sees it. Your Play Games name, avatar, achievements and scores can be seen by other players as your Play Games profile settings allow (everyone, friends only, or only you).
  • Your choices. You can play without signing in. You can delete your Play Games data for Space Cats, or your whole Play Games profile, in your Google Play Games profile settings or your Google Account.

Online play (optional)

ONLINE in the main menu lets you play ranked fights against other players over the internet, or against an "AI ghost" when nobody near your level is online. It uses our server, which runs on Cloudflare's network (Cloudflare, Inc. hosts it for us and processes this data only to run it). You can play everything else without ever opening ONLINE, and nothing is sent to our server until you do.

  • Your online account. The first time you open ONLINE, the game makes an account for you: no name, e-mail, password or sign-in. It is a random id, a random number shown as your #tag (like #4821), a random handle other players' games use to name you in a report or a block, and a secret key that stays in the game's save on your device; our server keeps only a scrambled fingerprint of the key. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the account is also linked to your Gausaa Games account (below).
  • What our server keeps. For the account: your #tag, the title, avatar frame and profile banner you chose in the game, how many fights you have played and arcade runs you have cleared (for your profile card), your Google Play Games name (only if you are signed in to Play Games: the name on your Play Games profile, never your Google account's own name), the fighter and skin you last searched with, your ranked points, a hidden skill rating, your wins, losses and streak, this season's wins, losses, best streak and best result, the season's rank road rewards already paid and your promotion shield, the ranked titles and frames you earned, your ranked wins and fights with each fighter (and the points won with each this season), your final and best points in past seasons, and an unclaimed season reward. For each ranked fight: which accounts fought with which fighters, who won, how the points changed, how it ended and when. Your best scores for the leaderboard: arcade score, survival run and combo, this season's and of all time, overall and with each fighter, with the fighter and skin of each and when it was made. These go to our server only after you have accepted the online rules; the first time, that includes the records already in your game.
  • During a fight against another player. Your fighter, colours, skin, title, Play Games name, avatar frame and aura, and the game version pass through our server to the other player's device, which computes the same fight. Your button presses go straight to the other player's device where your two networks allow it (a direct, encrypted connection between the two devices), and otherwise through our server too. None of it is stored.
  • Who sees it. Other players see your Play Games name (or, without one, your title and #tag), your title, avatar frame, profile banner, fighter and skin, your league and points, your ranked wins and losses, how many fights you have played and arcade runs you have cleared, your win streak, your mastery of each fighter, your best scores and your places on the leaderboard and on each fighter's list, in matches and on the leaderboard. When nobody near a player's level is online, your fighter, skin, title and league may be used for an AI ghost that the computer plays against them, and theirs for yours; the game always says when an opponent is an AI ghost.
  • Reports and blocks. If you REPORT another player, our server keeps who reported whom, the reason (their name, or cheating), the name at that moment and when, for at most 90 days, so that we can look into it; we do not tell the reported player who it was. A name reported by several players, or one we find breaks the rules, is hidden from everyone. If you BLOCK a player, our server keeps that until you lift it (ONLINE > ACCOUNT) or either account is deleted: you are not matched with them again and their name is not shown to you.
  • Your network address. Like any server, ours sees your device's internet address while you play online. We use it only to limit how many new accounts one address can make in an hour, keep it in memory for at most that hour and never store it. For the direct connection, the two devices in a fight against another player tell each other their network addresses (internet and local network) through our server, so the other player's device learns yours, as in any game that connects players directly; the game neither shows nor keeps it. Cloudflare's STUN service, which tells your device its internet address for this, sees it too. Cloudflare keeps its own request logs as described in its privacy policy.
  • Why. Only to run online play: matching you with players near your level, settling fights fairly, keeping the ladder and the seasons, and acting on reports. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising.
  • How long. As long as the account exists. Accounts not used for a year are deleted, and fights older than a year too. Deleting your account (below) removes it at once, with the reports and blocks by or about it.
  • Security. Everything between the game and our server is encrypted (HTTPS), and so is the direct connection between two devices (DTLS, as in every WebRTC connection).

Your Gausaa Games account (optional)

Gausaa Games makes more than one game. Through Google Play Games you get one account for all of them, so your online account comes back on a new phone, and our other games can recognise you as they get online play.

  • When. Only if you are signed in to Google Play Games when you open ONLINE. The game then asks Google Play Games for a one-time code and sends it to our Gausaa ID service, which gets from Google two ids of your Play Games account - your player id in Space Cats, and a key that is the same in every game Gausaa Games publishes on Google Play - and the name on your Play Games profile, which it passes on to our server (it does not keep it) so that other players see that name instead of your title and #tag. Google tells us nothing else: no e-mail, picture, achievements or scores, and never your Google account's own name. You sign in to nothing new, and if you are not signed in to Play Games none of this happens and your online account shows only your title and #tag.
  • What is kept. Gausaa ID keeps a random id for your Gausaa Games account, scrambled fingerprints of the two Play Games ids, which of our games you used it in, and when it was made and last used. Our Space Cats server keeps which Gausaa Games account your online account is linked to, and a scrambled fingerprint of the key of each other phone that signed in to it.
  • Why. Only to recognise you: to link your online account to your Play Games account, to give it back to a new phone or after a reinstall when you sign in to the same Play Games account, and to let our other games recognise the same player. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising.
  • Where. Gausaa ID runs on Cloudflare's network, like our game server (Cloudflare, Inc. hosts it for us and processes this data only to run it). Google handles the Play Games sign-in under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • How long. As long as you use it: a Gausaa Games account nobody signs in with for a year is deleted, with its links. Deleting it (below) removes it at once, together with the online accounts linked to it in all our games.

Cloud save (optional)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game asks once whether to keep your progress safe on our server; you can also turn CLOUD SAVE on, and off again, in Settings > Game. It is off until you say yes, and nothing is sent before that. With it on:

  • What our server keeps. Your Gausaa Games account's random id; your progress as the game saves it (your coins, what you unlocked and wear - fighters, skins, frames, banners, titles, music - your records, missions and daily and weekly progress; not your settings, and not your online account's key); the same coins and unlocked items as a list; your progress as it was at the start of each of the last 14 days; a scrambled fingerprint of a key for each phone you use it on; and, if you ever buy an item for real money, a record of that purchase: which item, when, and a scrambled fingerprint (SHA-256) of Google Play's purchase token. No name, e-mail address, device identifier, location or payment details: payments stay with Google Play.
  • Who sees it. Only you, through the game, and us.
  • Why. So that a new phone, or a second one, has your progress and your items when you sign in to the same Play Games account, and so that nothing you earned or bought is lost or counted twice. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising.
  • Where. In a database on Cloudflare's network, like our game server (Cloudflare, Inc. hosts it for us and processes this data only to run it), sent encrypted.
  • How long. Until you delete it. A cloud save nobody uses for a year is deleted.
  • Deleting it. See Deleting your data.

Rewarded videos (optional)

Space Cats shows an ad only when you ask for one. x2 COINS after a fight and GIFT on the DAILY screen each play a short video and pay coins when you watch it to the end. There are no banners and no ads between screens or during a fight, and you can play the whole game without ever watching a video.

  • Who shows them. Google, through its AdMob service, whose software is part of the game. We do not choose the ads and learn nothing about you from them: we see only totals, such as how many videos were watched.
  • What Google collects. From when the game opens, and when a video loads or plays, Google's ad software collects your device's advertising ID and Android's app set ID, your internet address (from which Google estimates your general location), how you use the ads and the game around them (the game opening, videos watched, taps on an ad) and diagnostics (crashes and performance). Google uses this to show and measure ads, to limit how often you see one, to prevent fraud and, where you allow it, to personalise ads, under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Your consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks you first, the first time the game opens, and you can change your answer at any time in the game: Settings > Game > AD PRIVACY CHOICES. If you do not consent, Google shows only ads that are not personalised, or none.
  • Your other choices. If you never tap x2 COINS or GIFT, no ad is ever shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads), which ends personalised ads in every app.
  • What kind of ads. The game asks Google only for ads rated for general audiences with parental guidance (PG).
  • Test versions. In test versions of the game the videos are Google's own test ads.

Wi-Fi battles

WI-FI BATTLE lets two players fight each other on two devices on the same local network (the same Wi-Fi, or one device sharing its hotspot). It only connects when you choose HOST or JOIN, and only directly to the other player's device at the address you enter. The two devices exchange only what the fight needs: button presses, fighter picks, the match settings, the game version and the look each player chose (title, avatar frame, aura colours, skin). None of it is stored by the game or sent anywhere else.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings and progress (coins, unlocked items, records and missions) in files on your device. These files never leave the device through the game, unless you turn the cloud save on (see Cloud save: then a copy of your progress, never your settings, is kept on our server); only the achievements and scores described under Google Play Games are sent to Google, while you are signed in. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include them in your own Google backup so your progress can come back on a new phone; that backup belongs to your Google account and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game deletes these files.

Permissions

The game itself uses three Android permissions:

  • vibration, for short feedback when you press a touch button. You can turn it off in the game: Settings > Controls > Vibration;
  • network access (Android's "internet" permission), for ONLINE play with our server, for WI-FI BATTLE, to connect to the other player's device on your local network, and for Google Play Games while you are signed in;
  • keeping the Wi-Fi awake (Android's "wake lock" permission), only while a WI-FI BATTLE or an online fight is connected, so the Wi-Fi answers quickly instead of saving power between button presses.

Google's ad software, which shows the rewarded videos, adds its own: access to the advertising ID and to Android's ad services (ad ID, attribution and topics), the network state (whether the device is online, before a video is loaded) and a foreground service for its background work. None of them asks you anything.

Deleting your data

Space Cats by Gausaa Games keeps data about you on a server only for online play and, if you turn it on, for the cloud save. You can delete all of it yourself, at any time, like this.

  • Your online account (your #tag, ranked points, league, best scores and fight history on our server): in the game, open ONLINE > ACCOUNT > DELETE ONLINE ACCOUNT and confirm. The account and its standing are deleted from our server at once, and your id is removed from the fights it was in (those fights stay counted for the other players). If you can no longer open the game, e-mail gausaa.games@gmail.com with your online name and #tag as the game shows them (on ONLINE > ACCOUNT, for example MIA #4821 or STREET CAT #4821) and we delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the game does not delete the online account; an account nobody uses for a year is deleted automatically.
  • Your Gausaa Games account (the link between your Play Games account and your online accounts in our games): in the game, open ONLINE > ACCOUNT > DELETE FROM ALL GAUSAA GAMES, or Settings > Game > CLOUD SAVE > DELETE FROM ALL GAUSAA GAMES if you use the cloud save, and confirm. It is deleted at once, and so are the online accounts linked to it in every Gausaa Games game, this one's included. If you can no longer open the game, e-mail gausaa.games@gmail.com with your online name and #tag as the game shows them (on ONLINE > ACCOUNT) and we delete it within 30 days. The steps for all our games are also at mobahug.github.io/gausaa/delete.html.
  • Your cloud save (the copy of your progress on our server, if you turned it on): in the game, open Settings > Game > CLOUD SAVE > DELETE CLOUD SAVE and confirm. It is deleted from our server at once, with its daily copies and purchase fingerprints; your progress on the device stays, and the cloud save is off. Deleting your online account (when it is linked to your Gausaa Games account) or your Gausaa Games account deletes it too. Another phone that still had the cloud save on does not bring it back: it turns its own off. If you can no longer open the game on that phone: install it again, sign in to the same Play Games account and delete it there; or, if you have an online account linked to the same Gausaa Games account, e-mail gausaa.games@gmail.com with your online name and #tag, as above, and we delete both within 30 days. A cloud save nobody uses for a year is deleted automatically.
  • Your Google Play Games data for Space Cats (achievements and leaderboard scores):
    • on Android, open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile icon (top right), then Settings > General > Play Games Profile > Privacy & Settings; under "Your Data" tap Delete profile & game data, then under "Delete individual game data" find Space Cats and tap Delete;
    • on the web, go to play.google.com/games/profile, click Your Data > Delete Play Games Account & Data, then Delete next to Space Cats.
    The same page can delete your whole Play Games profile. Google deletes this data permanently (Google's help page).
  • Your progress and settings on your device: uninstall Space Cats, or clear its storage (Android's Settings > Apps > Space Cats > Storage > Clear storage). They are deleted at once.
  • Your Android backup of the game, if Android backup is on: it belongs to your Google Account, where you can delete your device backups (Google Drive > Backups).
  • What Google's ad software collected for the rewarded videos: Google holds it, not us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads) and, where Google's consent form applies, change your answer in the game (Settings > Game > AD PRIVACY CHOICES).

Once your online account, your Gausaa Games account and your cloud save are deleted, nothing about you is retained on our side. Google may keep data as the Google Privacy Policy describes. Questions: gausaa.games@gmail.com.

Children

Space Cats is not directed at children under 13. Online play collects only the online account, the best scores, the ranked results and, with Google Play Games, the account link and the Play Games name described above, and lets nobody type messages or names. Google Play Games, if used, is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. The rewarded videos are Google's (see Rewarded videos): they play only when the player taps for one, with ads rated PG at most.
