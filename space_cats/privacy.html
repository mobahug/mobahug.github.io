Space Cats Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Space Cats is a game by Gausaa Games ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with information about you. To delete your data, see Deleting your data.

What comes to us

Space Cats has:

  • no ads and no advertising ID,
  • no analytics or tracking,
  • no in-app purchases,
  • no names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, contacts, location or device identifiers collected.

The only thing that comes to us is what ONLINE play needs, and only if you open ONLINE: a nameless online account and its ranked fights (see Online play). The rest of what can leave your device is described below: your achievements and scores, only if you use Google Play Games, and what a Wi-Fi battle sends to the other player's device.

Google Play Games (optional)

On Android, Space Cats uses Google Play Games Services for achievements and leaderboards. You can play the whole game without it.

  • Signing in. If you have a Google Play Games profile, Play Games may sign you in when the game starts. If you are not signed in, the game works just the same, and you can sign in later from PROGRESS (the trophy in the main menu), on its MISSIONS or RECORDS tab.
  • What is sent. While you are signed in, the game sends Google your achievements (the missions you finish and your progress towards them) and your scores on three leaderboards (arcade high score, best survival run and best combo), together with your Play Games player ID. The Play Games software in the game also sends Google technical information (diagnostics) to keep the service working.
  • Who receives it. This information goes to Google, which runs Google Play Games under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive it: we only see the leaderboards, like any player, and totals such as how many players earned an achievement.
  • Who sees it. Your Play Games name, avatar, achievements and scores can be seen by other players as your Play Games profile settings allow (everyone, friends only, or only you).
  • Your choices. You can play without signing in. You can delete your Play Games data for Space Cats, or your whole Play Games profile, in your Google Play Games profile settings or your Google Account.

Online play (optional)

ONLINE in the main menu lets you play ranked fights against other players over the internet, or against an "AI ghost" when nobody near your level is online. It uses our server, which runs on Cloudflare's network (Cloudflare, Inc. hosts it for us and processes this data only to run it). You can play everything else without ever opening ONLINE, and nothing is sent to our server until you do.

  • Your online account. The first time you open ONLINE, the game makes an account for you: no name, e-mail, password or sign-in. It is a random id, a random number shown as your #tag (like #4821) and a secret key that stays in the game's save on your device; our server keeps only a scrambled fingerprint of the key.
  • What our server keeps. For the account: your #tag, the title and avatar frame you chose in the game, the fighter and skin you last searched with, your ranked points, a hidden skill rating, your wins, losses and streak, this season's wins, losses, best streak and best result, the season's rank road rewards already paid and your promotion shield, the ranked titles and frames you earned, your ranked wins and fights with each fighter (and the points won with each this season), your final and best points in past seasons, and an unclaimed season reward. For each ranked fight: which accounts fought with which fighters, who won, how the points changed, how it ended and when.
  • During a fight against another player. Your fighter, colours, skin, title, avatar frame and aura, and the game version pass through our server to the other player's device, which computes the same fight. Your button presses go straight to the other player's device where your two networks allow it (a direct, encrypted connection between the two devices), and otherwise through our server too. None of it is stored.
  • Who sees it. Other players see your title, #tag, avatar frame, fighter and skin, your league and points, your win streak, your mastery of the fighter you play and your places on the ladder and on that fighter's ladder, in matches and on the ladders. When nobody near a player's level is online, your fighter, skin, title and league may be used for an AI ghost that the computer plays against them, and theirs for yours; the game always says when an opponent is an AI ghost.
  • Your network address. Like any server, ours sees your device's internet address while you play online. We use it only to limit how many new accounts one address can make in an hour, keep it in memory for at most that hour and never store it. For the direct connection, the two devices in a fight against another player tell each other their network addresses (internet and local network) through our server, so the other player's device learns yours, as in any game that connects players directly; the game neither shows nor keeps it. Cloudflare's STUN service, which tells your device its internet address for this, sees it too. Cloudflare keeps its own request logs as described in its privacy policy.
  • Why. Only to run online play: matching you with players near your level, settling fights fairly, keeping the ladder and the seasons. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising.
  • How long. As long as the account exists. Accounts not used for a year are deleted, and fights older than a year too. Deleting your account (below) removes it at once.
  • Security. Everything between the game and our server is encrypted (HTTPS), and so is the direct connection between two devices (DTLS, as in every WebRTC connection).

Wi-Fi battles

WI-FI BATTLE lets two players fight each other on two devices on the same local network (the same Wi-Fi, or one device sharing its hotspot). It only connects when you choose HOST or JOIN, and only directly to the other player's device at the address you enter. The two devices exchange only what the fight needs: button presses, fighter picks, the match settings, the game version and the look each player chose (title, avatar frame, aura colours, skin). None of it is stored by the game or sent anywhere else.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings and progress (coins, unlocked items, records and missions) in files on your device. These files never leave the device through the game; only the achievements and scores described under Google Play Games are sent to Google, while you are signed in. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include them in your own Google backup so your progress can come back on a new phone; that backup belongs to your Google account and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game deletes these files.

Permissions

The game uses three Android permissions:

  • vibration, for short feedback when you press a touch button. You can turn it off in the game: Settings > Controls > Vibration;
  • network access (Android's "internet" permission), for ONLINE play with our server, for WI-FI BATTLE, to connect to the other player's device on your local network, and for Google Play Games while you are signed in;
  • keeping the Wi-Fi awake (Android's "wake lock" permission), only while a WI-FI BATTLE or an online fight is connected, so the Wi-Fi answers quickly instead of saving power between button presses.

Deleting your data

Space Cats by Gausaa Games keeps data about you on a server only for online play. You can delete all of it yourself, at any time, like this.

  • Your online account (your #tag, ranked points, league and fight history on our server): in the game, open ONLINE > ACCOUNT > DELETE ONLINE ACCOUNT and confirm. The account and its standing are deleted from our server at once, and your id is removed from the fights it was in (those fights stay counted for the other players). If you can no longer open the game, e-mail gausaa.games@gmail.com with your online name (title and #tag, as shown in ONLINE > ACCOUNT) and we delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the game does not delete the online account; an account nobody uses for a year is deleted automatically.
  • Your Google Play Games data for Space Cats (achievements and leaderboard scores):
    • on Android, open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile icon (top right), then Settings > General > Play Games Profile > Privacy & Settings; under "Your Data" tap Delete profile & game data, then under "Delete individual game data" find Space Cats and tap Delete;
    • on the web, go to play.google.com/games/profile, click Your Data > Delete Play Games Account & Data, then Delete next to Space Cats.
    The same page can delete your whole Play Games profile. Google deletes this data permanently (Google's help page).
  • Your progress and settings on your device: uninstall Space Cats, or clear its storage (Android's Settings > Apps > Space Cats > Storage > Clear storage). They are deleted at once.
  • Your Android backup of the game, if Android backup is on: it belongs to your Google Account, where you can delete your device backups (Google Drive > Backups).

Once your online account is deleted, nothing about you is retained on our side. Google may keep data as the Google Privacy Policy describes. Questions: gausaa.games@gmail.com.

Children

Space Cats is not directed at children under 13. Online play collects only the nameless account and ranked results described above, nothing that identifies a person directly, and lets nobody type messages or names. Google Play Games, if used, is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
