Purrsuit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Purrsuit is a cozy cat runner game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

What we collect

Nothing ourselves. Purrsuit has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking of its own, and we don't collect, store or share any personal information on any server. The only exception is the optional video ads described below, which are served by Google.

Optional video ads

You can choose to watch a short video ad for a Paw Gem, or to continue a run. Videos only play when you tap to watch one. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. The videos come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as its advertising ID, an approximate location based on your IP address, and diagnostic data. This happens when the game gets a video ready and when you watch one. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ads are prepared. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your Android device settings. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

What stays on your device

Your progress (fish, Paw Gems, cats, upgrades, lounge decorations, settings and statistics) is saved on your own device. Android may include it in your device's own backup to your Google account (Android Auto Backup), so it can be restored if you reinstall the game; you can turn backups off in Android's settings. We never receive this data.

In-app purchases

Paw Gem packs and the Starter Pack are sold through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google: we never see your payment details. Google Play tells the game which product was bought so it can deliver it. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Purrsuit is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
