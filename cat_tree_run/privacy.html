Purrsuit Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Purrsuit is a cozy cat runner game for Android, made by Gausaa Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it, who handles it, and the choices you have.

In short: most of Purrsuit stays on your device. The optional LEADERBOARD and lounge visits use our own small server: if you choose to show your Cat Lounge there, other players can see your Google Play Games name, your lounge and your best distances. The optional video ads, the purchases and the optional Google Play Games achievements are handled by Google, as described below. We run no analytics or tracking of our own. To delete your data, see Deleting your data.

What we collect

Only what the optional LEADERBOARD needs, and only after you open it and agree to the lounge rules (see Online lounges and the LEADERBOARD). We never ask for your e-mail address or your real name, and we never sell your information. Everything else that leaves your device goes to Google: for the optional video ads, for purchases, and for Google Play Games if you use it.

Optional video ads (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a short video ad to get a Paw Gem or to continue a run. Videos only play when you tap to watch one. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play.

The videos come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as its advertising ID, an approximate location based on your IP address, information about the device and the app, and how you interact with the ads. This happens when the game gets a video ready and when you watch one. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form before any ads are prepared, and ads are shown according to your choice. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your Android device settings. We ask Google to limit the ads to content suitable for general audiences (Google's PG rating or lower).

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Paw Gem packs and the Starter Pack are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment: we never see your payment details. Google Play tells the game which product was bought, so the game can deliver it and confirm the purchase to Google. This confirmation stays on your device and is not sent to us. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play Games (optional)

On Android, Purrsuit uses Google Play Games for achievements and leaderboards, and to sign you in to the LEADERBOARD. You can play the whole game without it.

  • Signing in. If you have a Google Play Games profile, Play Games may sign you in when the game starts. If you are not signed in, the game works just the same, and you can sign in later with the PLAY GAMES button on the Trophies screen or the sign-in line on the LEADERBOARD.
  • What is sent. While you are signed in, the game sends Google Play Games the trophies you earn (as achievements), your progress towards the trophies that count something (such as fish collected or ziplines ridden), and the score and distance of your runs and the distance of your Daily Runs (on three leaderboards), together with your Play Games player ID. Trophies and records earned while you were signed out are sent the next time you sign in. The Play Games software in the game also sends Google technical information (diagnostics and usage) to keep the service working.
  • Who receives it. This information goes to Google, which runs Google Play Games under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive it: we only see the leaderboards, like any player, and totals such as how many players earned an achievement. The game shows no Google leaderboard itself; you can open them in Google Play Games. Only if you use the LEADERBOARD does our server receive anything from your Play Games account: what, and why, is in the next section.
  • Who sees it. Your Play Games name, picture, achievements and scores can be seen by other players as your Play Games profile settings allow (everyone, friends only, or only you).
  • Your choices. You can play without signing in, and you can delete your Play Games data for Purrsuit, or your whole Play Games profile, at any time (see Deleting your data).

Online lounges and the LEADERBOARD (optional)

Nothing is sent before you open the LEADERBOARD and agree to the lounge rules. Anyone can then look at the boards and visit lounges. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game also signs you in to your Gausaa Games account (the one account for our games, kept by our Gausaa ID service: it receives two ids of your Play Games account, your player id in Purrsuit and a key that is the same in all our games, keeps them only as scrambled fingerprints, and passes your Play Games name on to our server at each sign-in).

  • What our server keeps: your Gausaa Games account id; your Google Play Games name (never your Google account's own name); if you choose SHOW MY LOUNGE, your Cat Lounge as plain data (its rooms, the decorations and where they stand, your cats and their collars; never the name you gave the lounge); your best distance this week and of all time; the paw prints you leave in other lounges and that visitors leave in yours, with how many pets each cat got; the reports you send and the players you block; when you last used it. No e-mail, location or device id, and no purchases or other progress unless you turn the cloud save on (below).
  • Who sees it: other players see your Play Games name, your runner cat, your lounge, its coziness, your distances and how many paws it has. A player whose lounge you leave a paw in sees your name and the pets you gave. Reports are seen only by us.
  • Why: to show the boards and the lounges to other players, and to keep the game kind (reports and blocks).
  • Where: on Cloudflare, which runs our server and Gausaa ID for us (see Cloudflare's privacy policy; it keeps its own request logs). Your device's internet address reaches the server like any website's, and we do not keep it.
  • How long: until you delete it. Paw prints go after 30 days and reports after 90, and an account nobody uses for a year is deleted.
  • Deleting: see Deleting your data.

Cloud save (optional)

If you turn CLOUD SAVE on in Settings (it is off until you do, and it needs Google Play Games), your progress is also kept on our server under your Gausaa Games account, so that a new phone, or a second one, has it.

  • What our server keeps: your Gausaa Games account id; your progress as the game saves it (fish, Paw Gems, cats, collars, upgrades, lounge decorations and where they stand, missions and statistics; not your settings or your photos); the same fish, gems and owned things as a list; your save as it was at the start of each of the last 14 days; and for each in-app purchase a fingerprint (SHA-256) of Google Play's purchase token, which pack it was and when, so that what you bought is never lost or counted twice. No payment details: those stay with Google Play.
  • Who sees it: only you, through the game, and us.
  • Why: so that you keep your progress and what you bought when you change or lose your phone.
  • Where: on Cloudflare, as for the online lounges above.
  • How long: until you delete it. A cloud save nobody uses for a year is deleted.
  • Deleting: see Deleting your data.

What stays on your device

Your progress (fish, Paw Gems, cats, collars, upgrades, lounge decorations, missions and statistics), your settings and the photos you take in photo mode are saved on your device, in the game's own storage (your progress also on our server only if you turn the cloud save on, as above). Android may include the game's save in your device's backup to your Google account (Android Auto Backup), so it can be restored if you reinstall the game; you can turn backups off in Android's settings. Without the cloud save we never receive this data. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android's settings, deletes it from your device.

Deleting your data

Purrsuit by Gausaa Games keeps data about you on a server of ours only if you use the LEADERBOARD. You can delete all of it, and what the game leaves with you and with Google, yourself at any time:

  • Your LEADERBOARD data: in the game, LEADERBOARD > MY LOUNGE. SHOW MY LOUNGE off takes your lounge off the boards; TAKE MY LOUNGE OFFLINE deletes everything about you on Purrsuit's server; DELETE FROM ALL GAUSAA GAMES also deletes your Gausaa Games account and its online accounts in our other games. Without the game: use mobahug.github.io/gausaa/delete.html, or e-mail us with your name as the LEADERBOARD shows it; we delete it within 30 days.
  • Your Google Play Games data for Purrsuit (achievements and leaderboard scores): on Android, open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile icon (top right), then Settings > General > Play Games Profile > Privacy & Settings; under "Your Data" tap Delete profile & game data, then under "Delete individual game data" find Purrsuit and tap Delete. On the web, go to play.google.com/games/profile, click Your Data > Delete Play Games Account & Data, then Delete next to Purrsuit. The same page can delete your whole Play Games profile. Google deletes this data permanently (Google's help page).
  • Your cloud save: in the game, Settings > DELETE CLOUD SAVE (tap twice) deletes it from our server at once, with its daily copies and purchase fingerprints; your progress on the device stays. Deleting your Gausaa Games account deletes it too.
  • Your progress, settings and photos on your device: uninstall Purrsuit, or clear its storage (Android's Settings > Apps > Purrsuit > Storage > Clear storage). They are deleted at once.
  • Your Android backup of the game, if Android backup is on: it belongs to your Google Account, where you can delete your device backups (Google Drive > Backups).
  • Ad data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings, and use My Ad Center. Purchase records are kept by Google Play in your Google Account.

Google may keep data as the Google Privacy Policy describes. Questions: gausaa.games@gmail.com.

Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where your consent is required, ads are prepared and shown on the basis of the consent you give in Google's consent form, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Purchases are processed to perform the contract for the item you buy. Google Play Games is used only if you choose to sign in to it, to provide the achievements and leaderboards you ask for. The LEADERBOARD's data is processed to provide the boards and lounge visits you choose to use after agreeing to the lounge rules, and to keep them kind (reports and blocks).

Your rights

We hold the data above only while you use the LEADERBOARD: you can see it in the game, delete it there, or ask us by e-mail to look it up, correct it, export it or delete it. For the data Google processes for ads, purchases and Play Games, you can use the steps in Deleting your data and the controls in your Google Account and My Ad Center. You can always contact us with any privacy question, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Purrsuit is not directed at children under 13. The LEADERBOARD shows only names from Google Play Games and lounges built from the game's own pieces: nobody can type messages to anyone.
