Purrsuit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Purrsuit is a cozy cat runner game for Android, made by Gausaa Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it, who handles it, and the choices you have.

In short: Purrsuit has no accounts and no logins. We run no servers, analytics or tracking of our own, and we never receive your game data: your progress stays on your device. The optional video ads and the purchases are handled by Google, as described below.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't ask for your name, email address or any other personal information, and we don't collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server. The only information that leaves your device goes to Google, for the optional video ads and for purchases.

Optional video ads (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a short video ad to get a Paw Gem or to continue a run. Videos only play when you tap to watch one. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play.

The videos come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as its advertising ID, an approximate location based on your IP address, information about the device and the app, and how you interact with the ads. This happens when the game gets a video ready and when you watch one. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form before any ads are prepared, and ads are shown according to your choice. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your Android device settings. We ask Google to limit the ads to content suitable for general audiences (Google's PG rating or lower).

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Paw Gem packs and the Starter Pack are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment: we never see your payment details. Google Play tells the game which product was bought, so the game can deliver it and confirm the purchase to Google. This confirmation stays on your device and is not sent to us. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play Games (optional)

If your version of the game offers Google Play Games and you are signed in to it, the game sends your trophies and your best distances and scores to Google Play Games, so they appear as achievements and on leaderboards. Your Play Games profile is managed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can change its visibility in the Google Play Games settings.

What stays on your device

Your progress (fish, Paw Gems, cats, collars, upgrades, lounge decorations, missions and statistics), your settings and the photos you take in photo mode are saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. Android may include the game's save in your device's backup to your Google account (Android Auto Backup), so it can be restored if you reinstall the game; you can turn backups off in Android's settings. We never receive this data. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android's settings, deletes it from your device.

Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where your consent is required, ads are prepared and shown on the basis of the consent you give in Google's consent form, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Purchases are processed to perform the contract for the item you buy.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to look up, correct, export or delete. For the data Google processes for ads, purchases and Play Games, you can use the controls in your Google Account and My Ad Center. You can always contact us with any privacy question, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Purrsuit is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
