Purrsuit Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Purrsuit is a cozy cat runner game for Android, made by Gausaa Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it, who handles it, and the choices you have.

In short: Purrsuit has no accounts of its own. We run no servers, analytics or tracking of our own, and we never receive your game data: your progress stays on your device. The optional video ads, the purchases and the optional Google Play Games achievements and leaderboards are handled by Google, as described below. To delete your data, see Deleting your data.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't ask for your name, email address or any other personal information, and we don't collect, store, sell or share personal information on any server. The only information that leaves your device goes to Google: for the optional video ads, for purchases, and for Google Play Games if you use it.

Optional video ads (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a short video ad to get a Paw Gem or to continue a run. Videos only play when you tap to watch one. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play.

The videos come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as its advertising ID, an approximate location based on your IP address, information about the device and the app, and how you interact with the ads. This happens when the game gets a video ready and when you watch one. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form before any ads are prepared, and ads are shown according to your choice. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your Android device settings. We ask Google to limit the ads to content suitable for general audiences (Google's PG rating or lower).

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Paw Gem packs and the Starter Pack are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment: we never see your payment details. Google Play tells the game which product was bought, so the game can deliver it and confirm the purchase to Google. This confirmation stays on your device and is not sent to us. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play Games (optional)

On Android, Purrsuit uses Google Play Games for achievements and leaderboards. You can play the whole game without it.

  • Signing in. If you have a Google Play Games profile, Play Games may sign you in when the game starts. If you are not signed in, the game works just the same, and you can sign in later with the PLAY GAMES or LEADERBOARDS button on the Trophies screen.
  • What is sent. While you are signed in, the game sends Google Play Games the trophies you earn (as achievements), your progress towards the trophies that count something (such as fish collected or ziplines ridden), and the score and distance of your runs and the distance of your Daily Runs (on three leaderboards), together with your Play Games player ID. Trophies and records earned while you were signed out are sent the next time you sign in. The Play Games software in the game also sends Google technical information (diagnostics and usage) to keep the service working.
  • Who receives it. This information goes to Google, which runs Google Play Games under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive it: we only see the leaderboards, like any player, and totals such as how many players earned an achievement. The game does not read your Play Games name, picture or player ID.
  • Who sees it. Your Play Games name, picture, achievements and scores can be seen by other players as your Play Games profile settings allow (everyone, friends only, or only you).
  • Your choices. You can play without signing in, and you can delete your Play Games data for Purrsuit, or your whole Play Games profile, at any time (see Deleting your data).

What stays on your device

Your progress (fish, Paw Gems, cats, collars, upgrades, lounge decorations, missions and statistics), your settings and the photos you take in photo mode are saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. Android may include the game's save in your device's backup to your Google account (Android Auto Backup), so it can be restored if you reinstall the game; you can turn backups off in Android's settings. We never receive this data. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android's settings, deletes it from your device.

Deleting your data

Purrsuit by Gausaa Games keeps no data about you on any server of ours, so there is nothing for us to delete. You can delete what the game leaves with you and with Google yourself, at any time:

  • Your Google Play Games data for Purrsuit (achievements and leaderboard scores): on Android, open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile icon (top right), then Settings > General > Play Games Profile > Privacy & Settings; under "Your Data" tap Delete profile & game data, then under "Delete individual game data" find Purrsuit and tap Delete. On the web, go to play.google.com/games/profile, click Your Data > Delete Play Games Account & Data, then Delete next to Purrsuit. The same page can delete your whole Play Games profile. Google deletes this data permanently (Google's help page).
  • Your progress, settings and photos on your device: uninstall Purrsuit, or clear its storage (Android's Settings > Apps > Purrsuit > Storage > Clear storage). They are deleted at once.
  • Your Android backup of the game, if Android backup is on: it belongs to your Google Account, where you can delete your device backups (Google Drive > Backups).
  • Ad data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings, and use My Ad Center. Purchase records are kept by Google Play in your Google Account.

Google may keep data as the Google Privacy Policy describes. Questions: gausaa.games@gmail.com.

Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where your consent is required, ads are prepared and shown on the basis of the consent you give in Google's consent form, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Purchases are processed to perform the contract for the item you buy. Google Play Games is used only if you choose to sign in to it, to provide the achievements and leaderboards you ask for.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to look up, correct, export or delete. For the data Google processes for ads, purchases and Play Games, you can use the steps in Deleting your data and the controls in your Google Account and My Ad Center. You can always contact us with any privacy question, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Purrsuit is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.
