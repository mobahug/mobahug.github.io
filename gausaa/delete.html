Delete your Gausaa Games account

Last updated: 14 November 2026

GAUSAA Games is the developer of Space Cats: Arcade Fighter, Purrsuit and our other games on Google Play. A player who is signed in to Google Play Games when they open online play in one of our games (today: Space Cats: Arcade Fighter's ONLINE, and Purrsuit's LEADERBOARD), or when they turn a game's CLOUD SAVE on, has one Gausaa Games account for all of them: it links the online account and the cloud save they have in each game to their Google Play Games account, so they come back on a new phone. This page explains how to delete it, and what goes with it.

What the account holds

  • The Gausaa Games account, kept by our Gausaa ID service: a random id, scrambled fingerprints of two ids of your Google Play Games account (your player id in the game and a key that is the same in all our games), which of our games you used it in, and when it was made and last used. It keeps no name, e-mail, picture or game progress: your Play Games name is only passed on to the game's server at each sign-in.
  • The online account linked to it in each game, kept by that game's server: in Space Cats your Google Play Games name as other players see it, #tag, ranked points, league, season progress and fight history (the Space Cats privacy policy lists all of it); in Purrsuit your Google Play Games name as other players see it, your Cat Lounge as other players can visit it (its rooms, decorations and cats), your best distances, and the paw prints left in lounges (the Purrsuit privacy policy lists all of it).
  • The cloud save linked to it in each game, only if you turned CLOUD SAVE on there, kept by that game's server: a copy of your progress in the game, what you own in it, and for each in-app purchase a fingerprint of Google Play's purchase token with what was bought and when (each game's privacy policy lists all of it).

Delete it in a game

  1. Open the game while signed in to the Google Play Games account the Gausaa Games account belongs to.
  2. In Space Cats: ONLINE > ACCOUNT > DELETE FROM ALL GAUSAA GAMES, or, if you use the cloud save, Settings > Game > CLOUD SAVE > DELETE FROM ALL GAUSAA GAMES, and confirm with YES.
  3. In Purrsuit: LEADERBOARD > MY LOUNGE > DELETE FROM ALL GAUSAA GAMES, or, if you use the cloud save, Settings > DELETE MY GAUSAA ACCOUNT, and tap it again to confirm.

The Gausaa Games account is deleted at once, and each of our games' servers deletes the online account and the cloud save linked to it (it is asked again for up to 30 days if it cannot be reached at that moment). To delete only one game's online account and keep the rest, use that game's own delete instead (in Space Cats: ONLINE > ACCOUNT > DELETE ONLINE ACCOUNT; in Purrsuit: LEADERBOARD > MY LOUNGE > TAKE MY LOUNGE OFFLINE). To delete only a game's cloud save: in Space Cats, Settings > Game > CLOUD SAVE > DELETE CLOUD SAVE; in Purrsuit, Settings > DELETE CLOUD SAVE.

Delete it without the game

E-mail gausaa.games@gmail.com with the game and your online name and #tag as the game shows them (in Space Cats on ONLINE > ACCOUNT, for example MIA #4821 or STREET CAT #4821; in Purrsuit your name on the LEADERBOARD, for example MIA). We delete the Gausaa Games account and the online accounts and cloud saves linked to it within 30 days and reply when it is done. If you only ever used a cloud save, there is no online name for us to find it by, and we keep no e-mail with it: install the game again, sign in to the same Google Play Games account and delete it there, as above.

What is kept

Nothing about you is kept by us once the account is deleted. Ranked fights you played stay counted for the other players, with your id removed from them. A Gausaa Games account nobody signs in with for a year is deleted automatically, and so is an online account or a cloud save nobody uses for a year. Your Google Play Games profile, achievements and scores belong to Google: you delete those in your Google Play Games settings (Google's help page).
