Delete your Gausaa Games account

Last updated: 3 November 2026

GAUSAA Games is the developer of Space Cats: Arcade Fighter and our other games on Google Play. A player who is signed in to Google Play Games when they open online play in one of our games (today: Space Cats: Arcade Fighter) has one Gausaa Games account for all of them: it links the online account they have in each game to their Google Play Games account, so it comes back on a new phone. This page explains how to delete it, and what goes with it.

What the account holds

  • The Gausaa Games account, kept by our Gausaa ID service: a random id, scrambled fingerprints of two ids of your Google Play Games account (your player id in the game and a key that is the same in all our games), which of our games you used it in, and when it was made and last used. It keeps no name, e-mail, picture or game progress: your Play Games name is only passed on to the game's server at each sign-in.
  • The online account linked to it in each game, kept by that game's server: in Space Cats your Google Play Games name as other players see it, #tag, ranked points, league, season progress and fight history (the Space Cats privacy policy lists all of it).

Delete it in a game

  1. Open the game while signed in to the Google Play Games account the Gausaa Games account belongs to.
  2. In Space Cats: ONLINE > ACCOUNT > DELETE FROM ALL GAUSAA GAMES, and confirm with YES.

The Gausaa Games account is deleted at once, and each of our games' servers deletes the online account linked to it (it is asked again for up to 30 days if it cannot be reached at that moment). To delete only one game's online account and keep the rest, use that game's own DELETE ONLINE ACCOUNT instead (in Space Cats: ONLINE > ACCOUNT > DELETE ONLINE ACCOUNT).

Delete it without the game

E-mail gausaa.games@gmail.com with the game and your online name and #tag as the game shows them (in Space Cats on ONLINE > ACCOUNT, for example MIA #4821 or STREET CAT #4821). We delete the Gausaa Games account and the online accounts linked to it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is kept

Nothing about you is kept by us once the account is deleted. Ranked fights you played stay counted for the other players, with your id removed from them. A Gausaa Games account nobody signs in with for a year is deleted automatically, and so is an online account nobody uses for a year. Your Google Play Games profile, achievements and scores belong to Google: you delete those in your Google Play Games settings (Google's help page).
